{{ 只想记笔记，也可以吗？ ‖ Can I use it just for notes? }}+

{{ 可以。切到「只记录」后，它继续转写，不再生成回答；结束会议后，再整理成纪要。你可以在上方演示里试一下。 ‖ Yes. Switch to notes-only mode to keep the transcript without answer suggestions. When the meeting ends, it becomes structured notes. Try the switch in the demo above. }}

{{ 对方会看到我的回答吗？ ‖ Can other people see my prompts? }}+

{{ 是否可见取决于你共享的内容。共享整个电脑屏幕时，屏幕上的提示可能被看到；可把提示放到未共享的手机上。它不承诺「隐身」或无法检测。使用前请取得同意并遵守所在场合的规则。 ‖ Visibility depends on what you share. Prompts on your computer may be visible during full-screen sharing; you can move them to an unshared phone. There is no claim of invisibility or undetectability. Obtain consent and follow the rules of the setting. }}

{{ 这些回答和纪要可以直接相信吗？ ‖ Can I rely on the answers and notes? }}+

{{ 它们是帮助你思考的建议。重要信息仍需核对资料和原话，尤其是数字、决定和待办。纪要里的出处与「待确认」标记，是给你检查的起点。 ‖ They are suggestions to help you think. Check important facts against the materials and original discussion, especially numbers, decisions and action items. Sources and “to confirm” labels give you a place to start. }}

{{ 常被问到的。 ‖ Asked often. }}

01{{ 对方会知道我在用它吗？ ‖ Will the other side know I’m using it? }}

{{ 它不往会议里发任何东西，只在你的屏幕或手机上显示。共享整个屏幕时，屏幕上的提词可能被看到，那时就把回答放到手机上。用不用、怎么用，请遵守会议的规则。 ‖ It sends nothing into the meeting; it only shows on your screen or phone. If you share your whole screen, prompts on it can be seen, so move the answer to your phone. Whether and how you use it, follow the rules of the meeting. }}

02{{ 用哪个会议软件都可以吗？ ‖ Does it work with any meeting app? }}

{{ 可以。它听的是 Mac 放出来的声音，不用知道你在用哪一个。 ‖ Yes. It listens to the sound your Mac plays, so it doesn’t need to know which app that is. }}

03{{ 大家在同一个房间开会呢？ ‖ What if we’re all in one room? }}

{{ 选“线下”，它只用麦克风，转写标成“现场”。 ‖ Choose In person. It uses only the microphone and marks the transcript In the room. }}

04{{ 会留下录音吗？ ‖ Does it keep recordings? }}

{{ 不会。声音在这台 Mac 上变成文字，留下的只有转写和纪要。 ‖ No. Speech becomes text on your Mac; only the transcript and notes remain. }}

05{{ 不想被打扰，只想要一份纪要？ ‖ I only want notes, no interruptions. }}

{{ 点“立即记录”。开会时它安静地记，散会后，纪要就在那一页上。 ‖ Press Record now. It takes notes quietly during the meeting, and the notes are on the page when it ends. }}

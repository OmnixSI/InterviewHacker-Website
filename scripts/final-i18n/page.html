  1. i.

    {{ 在这台 Mac 上听。 ‖ Heard on this Mac. }}

    {{ 对方和你说的话，默认就在本机变成文字。 ‖ What they say and what you say becomes text right here, by default. }}

  2. ii.

    {{ 不留录音。 ‖ No recordings. }}

    {{ 留下来的只有转写和纪要，想删就删。 ‖ Only the transcript and the notes remain, and you can delete them whenever you like. }}

    {{ 交给谁，你说了算。 ‖ You decide who sees what. }}

{{ 不做“隐身”承诺：录音和转写之前，请取得在场的人同意，并遵守会议的规则。 ‖ No stealth promises: ask the people in the room before you record or transcribe, and follow the rules of the meeting. }}

№ 08{{ 问答 ‖ FAQ }}

{{ 常被问到的。 ‖ Questions people ask. }}

{{ 对方会知道我在用它吗？ ‖ Will the other side know? }}

{{ 它不往会议里发任何东西，只在你的屏幕或手机上显示。共享整个屏幕时，屏幕上的提词可能被看到，那时就把回答放到手机上。用不用、怎么用，请遵守会议的规则。 ‖ It never sends anything into the meeting; it only shows things on your screen or phone. If you share your whole screen, the prompt on it may be seen, so move the answers to your phone. Whether and how to use it is up to the rules of the room. }}

{{ 用哪个会议软件都可以吗？ ‖ Does it work with any meeting app? }}

{{ 可以。它听的是 Mac 放出来的声音，不用知道你在用哪一个。 ‖ Yes. It listens to what your Mac plays, so it never needs to know which app the call is in. }}

{{ 大家在同一个房间开会呢？ ‖ What if we’re all in one room? }}

{{ 选“线下”，它只用麦克风，转写标成“现场”。纪要只在有人被点到名时，才把事情记到他头上。 ‖ Choose In person and it uses the microphone alone, labelling the transcript “Room”. The notes only put a task on someone who was named out loud. }}

{{ 会留下录音吗？ ‖ Does it keep recordings? }}

{{ 不会。声音在这台 Mac 上变成文字就散了，留下的只有转写和纪要。 ‖ No. The sound becomes text on your Mac and is gone; only the transcript and notes stay. }}

{{ 不想被打扰，只想要一份纪要？ ‖ I just want notes, no prompting. }}

{{ 点“立即记录”。开会时它安静地记，散会几分钟后，纪要就在那一页上。 ‖ Press Record now. It listens quietly through the meeting, and a few minutes after, the notes are on the page. }}

{{ 有英文界面吗？ ‖ Is it in English? }}

{{ 有，中英文随时切换。回答跟着对话的语言走，英文面试就用英文回答。 ‖ Yes, English and Chinese. Answers follow the conversation, so an English interview gets English answers. }}
